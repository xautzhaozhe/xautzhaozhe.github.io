Zhe Zhao

My name is Zhe Zhao. I received my Bachelor's and Master's degrees from Xi'an University of Technology in 2019 and 2022, respectively, and I began my doctoral studies at Xidian University in 2023. My research interests include infrared and hyperspectral image processing, as well as anomaly detection. I have authored or co-authored over ten papers, including PR, JAG, IPM, and IEEE JSTARS. In addition, I served as a reviewer for journals like IEEE TCSVT, IEEE TPAMI, JAG, PR, ESWA, KBS and IPM. I was awarded the National Scholarship for both Master's and Doctoral Students in 2021 and 2025, respectively.

我是赵哲，陕西商洛人。分别于2019年和2022年在西安理工大学取得工学学士和硕士学位，2022年7月到2023年6月在西安晶晨半导体有限公司从事深度学习算法工程师工作。自2023年9月起，我在西安电子科技大学攻读博士学位，研究方向主要是红外和高光谱图像处理以及异常检测。目前，以第一作者或学生第一作者发表论文十余篇，涵盖PR、JAG、IPM、IEEE JSTARS等期刊。同时，我也担任IEEE TCSVT、IEEE TPAMI、JAG、PR、IPM等多个期刊的审稿人。此外，我曾获得硕士研究生和博士研究生国家奖学金等荣誉。
